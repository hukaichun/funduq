## What it is

A central Delegation Authority Service that issues delegation tokens,
verifies intent before execution, intercepts calls at execution, and blocks
those outside scope. Tokens carry an authority scope, an intent vector, NIST
800-53 policy constraints, a parent hash and a DAS signature.

**The adjudicator row's contemporary instance**, and the clearest contrast
with a witness: it is a third party that rules.
