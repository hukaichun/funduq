# EU AI Act — automatic logging for high-risk systems

- **id** — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 12(1)
- **when** — the broader enforcement phase begins August 2026
- **where** — the regulation text (EUR-Lex). **No URL recorded yet**
- **status** — `unread` — **cited in §1 and sourced only from secondary pages**
- **read on** — never; identified 2026-08-25 from search results

## What it is

The obligation that high-risk AI systems technically permit automatic
recording of events over the system's lifetime, with logs retained for a
period appropriate to the purpose and **at least six months** unless other
Union or national law provides otherwise.

## How the draft uses it

§1 ¶1, one clause: the demand side arriving at the same time as the gap. It
is not load-bearing — the hook is OP3 — but it is the only line in the
introduction that says anyone outside the field is asking.

## Cautions

- **Quote the article, not a compliance blog.** Everything above came from
  secondary sources, and a misquoted regulation in an introduction is the
  cheapest possible way to lose a reviewer.
- Resist the surrounding commentary. Blogs describe the wanted audit trail as
  "append-only, hash-chained, tamper-evident", which is convenient for this
  paper and **is not what the article says**. Do not let a convenient
  paraphrase enter as a citation.
- Check whether Article 12 or Article 19 (or both) is the right anchor for
  the retention period, and whether "deployer" or "provider" carries the
  obligation — the draft should name whoever actually bears it.
